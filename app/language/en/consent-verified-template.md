# Additional consent — study with identity verification

This consent applies **in addition to** the app's general consent document. It
does not replace it.

## Why we know who you are

This study requires, for medical and regulatory reasons, that the study team
knows which person contributed which data — for example so that you can be
contacted if an adverse event occurs.

## What is collected, and where it is held

| Data                                     | Where it is stored                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Name, date of birth, contact details     | **A separate, encrypted identity register** held by the study site |
| Your study data (habits, questionnaires) | The platform's research database                                   |

The research database does **not** contain your name. It knows only a study
code such as `TUD-DFG01-0042`.

## Who can identify you, and under what conditions

Researchers analysing your study data **cannot** determine who you are.
Identification is possible only when **all** of the following hold:

1. there is a documented reason (for example a serious adverse event, a
   regulatory inspection, or your own request),
2. a **second person** approves — whoever raises the request may not approve it
   themselves,
3. the disclosure is **time-limited** and restricted to the named fields and to
   you alone.

**Every identification is permanently recorded** and can be audited afterwards.

## Withdrawal and erasure

You may withdraw your consent at any time, without giving reasons.

Importantly, so you know what that means: on withdrawal your entry in the
identity register is deleted. **Study data already collected is retained
pseudonymously** and continues to be analysed — but it can then no longer be
attributed to anyone, including by us. This deletion cannot be undone.

## Consent

By agreeing you confirm that you have read and understood the above and consent
to the identity verification described.
