## 1. Accessing the App

You can use Health Habit Hub on Android, iOS, or directly in your browser — no account registration required.

### Android (APK install)

**Step 1.** Download the latest APK file from the link provided by the study team (e.g. in your invitation email).

**Step 2.** On your Android phone, open **Settings → Security** and enable **Install from unknown sources** (or **Allow from this source** for your browser).

**Step 3.** Open the downloaded APK file and tap **Install**. Tap **Open** when installation completes.

**Step 1.** Use the link provided by the study team in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.

**Step 2.** Follow the on-screen instructions to install the app via TestFlight or open the browser version.

### Browser (Chrome / Firefox)

**Step 1.** Open Chrome or Firefox and navigate to the URL provided by your study coordinator (e.g. `https://hhh.tu-dresden.de`).

**Step 2.** You can optionally **Add to Home Screen** for quick access — tap the share icon in Safari/Chrome and choose **Add to Home Screen**.

## 2. Logging in with Your Token Card

The study team will give you a **token card** — a small card with a QR code and a printed username and password. You do not need to choose or remember a password.

**Step 1.** Open the app. You will see the **Health Habit Hub** login screen with a teal shield icon, a **Username** field, and a **Password** field.

**Step 2 (QR scan — Android/iOS only).** Tap the QR scan button if shown, then point your camera at the QR code on your token card. The username and password fields will fill in automatically.

**Step 3 (manual entry).** If QR scanning is not available or you are using a browser, type the username and password exactly as printed on your token card. Usernames look like `hhh-1234`.

**Step 4.** Tap **Login**. A loading spinner will appear briefly while the app connects to the server. You will then be taken to the main screen.

## 3. Completing Your Profile

Before you can donate habits or receive recommendations, you need to complete a short profile questionnaire. This helps us personalise your experience.

**Step 1.** After logging in, tap the **Profile** tab at the bottom of the screen (it looks like a person icon).

**Step 2.** The profile questionnaire will load inside the app. It asks a few short questions about your background — for example, your age group and how often you exercise. All answers are anonymous.

**Step 3.** Answer each question and tap **Next** to move to the following page.

**Step 4.** When you reach the last page, tap **Submit**. You will see a green tick and the message **Profile Completed** with today's date.

## 4. Donating a Habit

Donating a habit means sharing one of your everyday routines with the research project. For example: *"I brush my teeth after I wake up every morning"*. All donations are anonymous.

**Step 1.** Tap the **Donate** tab at the bottom of the screen (it looks like a gift icon).

**Step 2.** The habit-donation survey will load. Depending on which study group you are in, you may see detailed instructions or just a simple text box — this is intentional.

**Step 3.** Describe your habit in the text box. Be as specific as you can: include what you do, when you do it, and where.

**Step 4.** If prompted, use the buttons below the text box to mark words as **behaviour** (the action you perform) or **context** (when or where it happens). Tap a word or phrase in your text to select it, then tap the appropriate button.

**Step 5.** Tap **Submit** at the bottom. A confirmation message — **Habit donated successfully!** — will appear briefly.

## 5. Exploring the Habit Graph and Annotating Nodes

The **Explore** tab shows you an interactive graph of all the habits donated by participants. You can browse and annotate the nodes to help researchers classify habits.

**Step 1.** Tap the **Explore** tab at the bottom of the screen.

**Step 2.** The habit graph will load. You will see habit nodes grouped by category. Use the **category filter** at the top to narrow down the habits shown.

**Step 3.** Tap any habit node to open a detail view. You can read the habit description and see how other participants have annotated it.

**Step 4 (annotating).** In the detail view, tap the annotation buttons — for example **Behaviour** or **Context** — to classify part of the habit text. Your annotation is saved automatically.

**Step 5.** Scroll through the graph to discover more habits. You can zoom in and out by pinching on touch screens.

## 6. Understanding Your Recommendations

After you complete your profile and donate at least one habit, the **Recommend** tab shows personalised habit suggestions based on your profile and donated habits.

**Step 1.** Tap the **Recommend** tab at the bottom of the screen.

**Step 2.** A list of recommendation cards will appear. Each card shows:
- **Habit name** — a short description of the suggested habit.
- **Category** — the behaviour category (e.g. Exercise, Sleep, Nutrition).
- **Rationale** — why this habit was recommended for you, explained in plain language.
- **Citation** — the research reference that supports this recommendation.

**Step 3.** For each recommendation you can:
- Tap **Accept** to add it to your personal habit list.
- Tap **Dismiss** to remove it from your list.

New recommendations appear automatically when the system finds new matches — you will see a live indicator dot in the top-right corner of the screen (green = live, grey = polling every few minutes).

## 7. Updating Your Profile

You can update your profile at any time if your circumstances change.

**Step 1.** Tap the **Profile** tab at the bottom of the screen.

**Step 2.** If you have already completed your profile, you will see a **Profile Completed** card with a green tick and the date you filled it in.

**Step 3.** Tap the **Edit** button on the card.

**Step 4.** The profile questionnaire will open again with your previous answers pre-filled. Update any answers that have changed.

**Step 5.** Tap **Submit** when you are done. Your profile will be saved and the card will show the new completion date.
