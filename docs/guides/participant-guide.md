# Health Habit Hub — Participant Guide

Welcome to Health Habit Hub. This guide walks you through everything you need to get started as a study participant. It should take about five minutes to read.

---

## What You Need

- Your **token card** — given to you by the study coordinator. It has a QR code and a printed username and password.
- A smartphone or tablet (Android or iOS), or a web browser on your computer.
- An internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data).

---

## 1. First Login

**Step 1.** Open the Health Habit Hub app (or navigate to the URL provided by your coordinator).

**Step 2 — Scan the QR code (easiest).** Tap the QR scan button on the login screen, then point your camera at the QR code on your token card. The username and password will fill in automatically.

**Step 3 — Or enter manually.** If scanning does not work, type the username and password exactly as printed on your token card. Usernames look like `hhh-1234`. They are case-sensitive.

**Step 4.** Tap **Login**. The app will connect to the server and take you to the home screen.

> Keep your token card in a safe place. If you lose it, contact your study coordinator.

---

## 2. Donating a Habit

Donating a habit means sharing one of your everyday routines with the research project — for example: _"I drink a glass of water right after I wake up every morning."_ All donations are anonymous.

**Step 1.** Tap the **Donate** tab at the bottom of the screen.

**Step 2.** The habit survey will load. Follow the on-screen instructions to describe your habit.

**Step 3.** Tap **Submit**. You will see a confirmation message once your habit has been saved.

You can donate more than one habit — just tap the **Donate** tab again after each submission.

---

## 3. Viewing Your Habits

The **Explore** screen shows the habits that have been donated by participants.

**Step 1.** Tap the **Explore** tab at the bottom of the screen.

**Step 2.** Browse the habits shown. You can tap any habit to read more about it.

---

## 4. Settings

Tap the **Settings** icon (usually in the top corner of the screen) to access the following options:

- **Language** — switch the app's display language (English, German, French, Japanese, Dutch).
- **Legal** — tap **Settings → Legal** to read the terms of use, privacy policy, and imprint.

---

## 5. If Something Goes Wrong

| Problem                         | What to do                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cannot scan the QR code         | Enter the username and password from your token card manually                                 |
| App says "Invalid credentials"  | Double-check you typed the username and password exactly as printed (they are case-sensitive) |
| App will not connect            | Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data connection and try again                                      |
| Not sure which account is yours | Ask your study coordinator — they can look it up for you                                      |

---

## 6. Stopping Participation

You can stop using the app at any time — simply stop opening it. There is no need to delete your account or notify anyone. Any data you have already donated stays in the study in pseudonymous form, as described in the privacy policy (**Settings → Legal**).

---

## Questions?

If you have any questions or run into a problem not covered here, contact your study coordinator using the details on your token card or in your invitation email.

Thank you for taking part in the Health Habit Hub research project.
