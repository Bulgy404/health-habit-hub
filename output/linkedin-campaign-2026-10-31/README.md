# Health Habit Hub: ordinary days

Created 31 October 2026 and polished 1 November 2026. The calendar and habit-plan illustrations now follow the campaign’s typography, colours and square geometry.

Open `index.html` for the artwork, English/German post copy and the new 18-second motion post. All three PNGs are 1080 × 1350, suitable for portrait feed posts. SVG masters and fonts are included for editing.

`video/01-give-it-a-when.mp4` is an 18-second silent motion post with readable copy and genuine app footage. Pair it with the second post. The native phone footage came from the existing PromoStudio project; the new typography, timing and composition were authored for this revision.

The campaign uses specific everyday language, condensed poster lettering and purpose-drawn habit illustrations. The first poster’s multiweek heatmap uses binary recorded/no-entry states; the second illustrates a cue and action based on the app’s reading-habit example. Both are explicitly labelled illustrations, not app screenshots, participant records or study results. The video retains genuine app footage. Static artwork contains no generated lifestyle photography or invented handwriting.

The brand icon and product facts remain unchanged. Sharing habits for TU Dresden research is presented as a choice. Nothing has been published or scheduled.

Fonts: Barlow Condensed from [Google Fonts’ official repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed), licensed under the included SIL Open Font License. Figtree is the existing project body typeface. Source app image: `website/src/assets/device-mockups/Habits.png`.
