Ordinary week
Dark Health Habit Hub poster. Large text reads “Tuesday got away. Wednesday is still here.” An illustrative calendar uses acid-green square cells for recorded entries and outlined cells for no entry. It shows weekday rows without real dates or participant data. Free download for iOS and Android at healthhabithub.de.

Give it a when
Bright green Health Habit Hub poster. Large text reads “Give your habit a when.” A matching typographic illustration labelled “Example plan” reads “When I get into bed, I will read for ten minutes.” Free download for iOS and Android at healthhabithub.de.

Ordinary Tuesday
Light Health Habit Hub poster. Text reads “Researchers are interested in your ordinary Tuesday.” It mentions a walk after lunch and a book before bed, then invites people to track habits and choose whether to share them for TU Dresden research. Free download for iOS and Android at healthhabithub.de.
