Tuesday got away. Wednesday is still here.

Meetings ran over, the train was late, or the evening simply went differently. That’s an ordinary week.

Health Habit Hub gives you a place to record the habits you’re working on and see your entries in a calendar. Habit check-ins let you reflect on how a routine feels over time, even when the week looks uneven.

It’s a free app from TU Dresden’s Digital Health research group, available for iOS and Android. You can also choose to share habits for research.

Take a look and download the app:
https://healthhabithub.de/
