## 1. Tuesday got away. Wednesday is still here.

### English

Tuesday got away. Wednesday is still here.

Meetings ran over, the train was late, or the evening simply went differently. That’s an ordinary week.

Health Habit Hub gives you a place to record the habits you’re working on and see your entries in a calendar. Habit check-ins let you reflect on how a routine feels over time, even when the week looks uneven.

It’s a free app from TU Dresden’s Digital Health research group, available for iOS and Android. You can also choose to share habits for research.

Take a look and download the app:
[healthhabithub.de](https://healthhabithub.de/)

#HealthHabitHub #HabitTracking

Die App ist kostenlos, stammt von der Forschungsgruppe Digital Health an der TU Dresden und ist für iOS und Android verfügbar. Wenn du möchtest, teilst du außerdem Gewohnheiten für die Forschung.

Schau rein und lade die App herunter:

## 2. Give your habit a when.

### English

Give your habit a when.

“Read more” leaves a lot to decide at the end of the day.

In Health Habit Hub, a plan can look like this: “When I get into bed, I will read a book for ten minutes.”

You choose the habit and its cue, then use the calendar to record what actually happened. Habit check-ins let you reflect on how automatic the routine feels over time.

The app is free on iOS and Android, developed at TU Dresden. Sharing habits for research is optional.

If there’s a routine you’ve been meaning to make more concrete, take a look:
[healthhabithub.de](https://healthhabithub.de/)

#HealthHabitHub #Habits

## 3. Researchers are interested in your ordinary Tuesday.

### English

Researchers are interested in your ordinary Tuesday.

A walk after lunch or reading in bed might feel ordinary. Those everyday details are what habit research wants to understand.

Health Habit Hub, from TU Dresden’s Digital Health research group, lets you describe habits in your own words, record entries in a calendar and reflect on your routines through habit check-ins.

You can choose to share habits for research into how routines develop, change and persist. Your contribution starts with what you actually do in daily life.

The app is free for iOS and Android. Take a look:
[healthhabithub.de](https://healthhabithub.de/)

#HealthHabitHub #CitizenScience

### Deutsch

Die Forschung interessiert sich für deinen ganz normalen Dienstag.

Nach dem Mittagessen spazieren gehen oder im Bett lesen klingt alltäglich. Genau solche Details möchte die Gewohnheitsforschung besser verstehen.

Mit Health Habit Hub von der Forschungsgruppe Digital Health an der TU Dresden kannst du Gewohnheiten in eigenen Worten beschreiben, Einträge im Kalender verfolgen und mit regelmäßigen Check-ins über deine Routinen nachdenken.

Wenn du möchtest, teilst du Gewohnheiten für die Forschung dazu, wie Routinen entstehen, sich verändern und bestehen bleiben. Dein Beitrag beginnt bei dem, was du im Alltag tatsächlich machst.

Die App ist kostenlos für iOS und Android verfügbar. Schau sie dir an:
[healthhabithub.de](https://healthhabithub.de/)

#HealthHabitHub #CitizenScience
