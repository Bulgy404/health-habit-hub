## Video options

- `video/01-promostudio-product-voiced.mp4`: 24-second product film with genuine app screens, native PromoStudio phone motion and a fresh English ElevenLabs voiceover. Pair with post 02.
- `video/02-promostudio-product-silent.mp4`: the same product film without narration, with on-screen feature text and a download invitation.
- `video/03-elevenlabs-presenter.mp4`: approximately 22-second vertical introduction with a fictional AI presenter, real app cutaways, visible captions and a clean end card. Pair with post 01. It reuses the existing ElevenLabs presenter take in a corrected local edit.
- `video/product-captions.srt`: English captions for the voiced product film. Upload alongside the MP4 in LinkedIn's caption settings. The gallery uses the equivalent `.vtt` track.
- `video/elevenlabs-product-voiceover.mp3`: separate original narration for later edits. Voice: Lily – Velvety Actress. Model: Eleven v4. One generation request produced two candidates; the first is used.

Upload the chosen image or MP4 to LinkedIn and paste the matching text. All posts use https://healthhabithub.de/ as the download destination. Nothing has been published or scheduled.

## Provenance and review

The three static posters were created with built-in image generation. Lifestyle imagery is synthetic and does not depict actual participants or testimonials. The product poster is generated from a genuine Habits-screen reference, rather than being an untouched screenshot. Exact prompts are in `image-prompts.md`.

Videos use real app-screen previews. Displayed example numbers are not study results. The AI-presenter label is preserved. The source presenter preview is lower resolution than the delivered canvas; it was reused without generating another paid presenter take.

The previous Studio export contained an incorrectly timed title overlay and a blank ending. It was rebuilt from the clean original presenter preview and its audio before inclusion here. The original external project remains at https://elevenlabs.io/app/studio/5SC1rlnC6Shgzv8tLoy2; the corrected deliverable is the local MP4.

Feature and account wording was checked against the current product copy, privacy text and [public Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=de.felixreinsch.healthhabithub). No testimonials, participation numbers, medical outcomes or guarantees were invented. Habit sharing for research is described as a choice. Standard public accounts do not require a name, email or phone number; individual study protocols can have additional identity requirements.

Production proofs: `source/elevenlabs-voiceover-proof.png` and `video/promostudio-editor-proof.png`. Editable production sources and scripts are kept in `source/` and the video production subfolder.
