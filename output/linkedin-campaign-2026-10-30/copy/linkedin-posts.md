## 1. One small habit. A place to start.

One small habit. A place to start.

A short walk after lunch. Reading before bed. A few minutes of stretching.

What’s one routine you’d like to make room for?

Health Habit Hub gives you a place to record your habits, check in on them and see your progress in a calendar. You can turn an intention into a concrete “when this happens, I will do that” plan, then keep track of how it fits into your everyday life.

The app is free and comes from the Digital Health research group at TU Dresden. You can also choose to share habits for research, helping researchers understand the routines people describe in daily life.

Start with one habit that matters to you.

## 2. Make your progress visible.

### English

Make your progress visible.

What did your habits look like this week?

Health Habit Hub gives that question a place in your day. Record the routines you want to work on, log what you do and see your entries in a calendar. Habit check-ins help you reflect on how automatic a routine feels over time.

You can also explore AI-generated suggestions drawn from a knowledge base curated by researchers. They offer ideas for habits to try, with an explanation of the suggestion. These suggestions are for habit building and do not replace medical advice.

Developed at TU Dresden, Health Habit Hub is free. You can choose which habits to share for research, alongside using the app to follow your own routines.

Take a look, choose a habit and make your first entry.

## 3. Your everyday habits could help science.

### English

Your everyday habits could help science.

What does a habit look like outside a lab?

It might be a walk after lunch, a bedtime routine or something you do without thinking. Researchers want to understand how these routines develop, change and persist in everyday life.

Health Habit Hub brings that question into an app from TU Dresden’s Digital Health research group.

You can describe your habits in your own words, follow your entries in a calendar and reflect on your routines through habit check-ins. If you choose to share habits, your contribution can help research into how habits form.

The app is free. Standard accounts need no name, email address or phone number, and sharing habits is your choice.

Curious about your own routines, or about the science behind them?

Explore Health Habit Hub and download the app:
[healthhabithub.de](https://healthhabithub.de/)

#HealthHabitHub #CitizenScience #DigitalHealth

### Deutsch

Deine Alltagsgewohnheiten könnten der Wissenschaft helfen.

Wie sieht eine Gewohnheit außerhalb des Labors aus?

Vielleicht ist es der Spaziergang nach dem Mittagessen, eine Abendroutine oder etwas, das du ganz nebenbei machst. Forschende möchten verstehen, wie solche Routinen im Alltag entstehen, sich verändern und bestehen bleiben.

Health Habit Hub bringt diese Frage in eine App der Forschungsgruppe Digital Health an der TU Dresden.

Du kannst deine Gewohnheiten in eigenen Worten beschreiben, deine Einträge im Kalender verfolgen und mit regelmäßigen Check-ins über deine Routinen nachdenken. Wenn du Gewohnheiten teilst, kann dein Beitrag die Forschung zur Gewohnheitsbildung unterstützen.

Die App ist kostenlos. Für ein reguläres Konto brauchst du weder Namen noch E-Mail-Adresse oder Telefonnummer. Welche Gewohnheiten du teilst, entscheidest du selbst.
