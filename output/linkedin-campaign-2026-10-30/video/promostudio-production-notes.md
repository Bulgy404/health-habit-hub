# Product film production

Created 30 October 2026 in PromoStudio, then finished with brand typography.

- Editor: https://www.promostudio.cloud/app
- Local project: `HHH LinkedIn — Make your progress visible — Oct 30`
- Source project duplicated: `HHH Participants — native phone shots`
- Native phone shots: Hero Reveal, Hero Tilt Reveal, Screen-First Reveal, each set to 8 seconds.
- Real saved screen assets: `HHH-App-Habits-Screen.png`, `HHH-App-Graph-Screen-Intact.png`, `HHH-App-Recommendations-Screen.png`.
- Background set to deep green; silver phone finish.
- Native export: `promostudio-native-source.mp4`, 1080 × 1350, 30 fps, 24 seconds, H.264, silent.
- Finished film: `02-promostudio-product-silent.mp4`, same dimensions and duration.

The finished version retimes the native shots to 0–7 seconds (habits), 7–12 (shared habits), and 12–19 (AI suggestions). A full download card occupies 19–24 seconds. Real app screens are labelled as previews, with a non-medical-advice label on the AI suggestions scene.

All main copy is readable without audio. The source app images are preserved in native phone renders. The final text graphics are generated from the repository's Figtree and Fraunces fonts. Source script: `../source/finish_promostudio.py`.

Export completed and a local MP4 was verified. `promostudio-editor-proof.png` shows the completed export and dimensions. `product-preview.png` is the final habits frame. Other preview PNGs show research, suggestions, and the download card. No public post was published and no paid upgrade was used.
