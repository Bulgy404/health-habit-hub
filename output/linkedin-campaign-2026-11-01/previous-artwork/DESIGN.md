# Health Habit Hub campaign: ordinary days

Scope: the replacement LinkedIn artwork and its local review gallery only. The website, app identity and the earlier campaign remain unchanged.

Mode: Persuade. People scrolling LinkedIn should recognise a real situation, understand one concrete app feature, then find the free download.

Direction: conversational campus posters. Plainspoken observations set in assertive condensed lettering, with genuine app details as evidence. The memorable content is an interrupted week, the word “when,” and an ordinary Tuesday that interests researchers.

The user asked for more human, distinctive work and rejected the staged lifestyle photography, repeated cream/serif/green composition and generic wellness language. This revision uses no synthetic photography, fake handwriting, testimonials, invented study data, decorative graphs or rendered replacement app screens.

## Visual contract

- Barlow Condensed ExtraBold carries the implemented display voice. Figtree Regular/Bold carries the brand, explanations and controls. SemiBold font files are included for editing. No display serif.
- Ink `#172126`, cool white `#F5F7F2`, acid leaf `#C7F07A`, institutional action green `#2E8C00`. The acid field is a campaign extension of the participant green; it does not change global brand tokens.
- Square edges, large type, little furniture, within a 4:5 portrait composition (1080 × 1350). Existing app icon remains unchanged. No floating phone product render on the static posters.
- Each exported image is 1080 × 1350. Genuine calendar and reading-entry crops retain their source pixels and carry an app-preview label.
- First viewport of the gallery leads with the three finished artworks. No oversized sales hero, rounded promotional cards or repeated eyebrow labels. Text switches EN/DE and copies to the clipboard; downloadable text remains available if clipboard access fails.
- The gallery is one cool-light surface. Its one authored motion is a short reveal of the selected language text, disabled under reduced motion. Exported artwork is static.

## Three messages

1. “Tuesday got away. Wednesday is still here.” Ink field, contrasting sentence rhythm, a wide genuine calendar detail. It does not claim that particular preview data belongs to a Tuesday or a person.
2. “Give your habit a when.” Acid field, oversized concrete language, the actual reading habit and its situational cue from the app.
3. “Researchers are interested in your ordinary Tuesday.” Cool-white recruitment poster with the everyday word as its visual centre. Sharing remains a choice.

## Design derivation

The catalog's night-city and plankton systems lose everyday identification and product clarity. These are judgment references, not decorative motifs to borrow. No external catalog image was adopted.
