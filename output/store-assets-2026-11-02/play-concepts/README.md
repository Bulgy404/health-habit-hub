# Google Play asset concepts

English/German 1024 × 500 opaque feature graphics, with matching editable SVGs, and two 25-second portrait teaser concepts at 1080 × 1920, H.264, 30fps, 750 frames, silent. Matching teaser posters and validation results are included.

The actual shared Flutter app UI is the main content. It uses fictional examples from the current Xcode capture, with the iOS status bar removed and no Apple hardware or storefront badge. The approved green identity and knowledge-graph motifs frame the experience.

These remain concepts until the corresponding UI is checked on Android and native Android footage replaces the iOS source. No Android system image/AVD is installed on this workstation. Nothing has been uploaded to YouTube or Google Play. A Play preview is supplied through an eligible public or unlisted YouTube video rather than a direct MP4 upload.
