# Google Play feature graphic concepts

English/German 1024 × 500 opaque PNGs, with matching editable SVGs. The app experience takes priority over an enlarged icon; the approved graph motifs and green palette connect it to the rest of the identity.

The displayed habit widget is cropped from the new actual Xcode capture of the shared Flutter UI. The crop contains no iOS status bar, Apple device hardware, or storefront badge. It contains fictional example data.

These remain concepts until the corresponding widget is checked on Android. No Android system image/AVD is installed on this workstation, so genuine Android screenshots or a final Android-specific preview were not captured in this batch. No YouTube or Google Play upload has been made.
