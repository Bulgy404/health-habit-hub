## Codebase Patterns
- All infrastructure branches (feature/traeffic, feature/h3-proxy, feature/server, docker-env, max-base-path, max-deployment-optionen) were already merged into master before this project started — merges into rapid_dev will be no-ops
- docker-compose config exits 0 cleanly on master/rapid_dev
- Real conflict markers use 7 angle brackets: `<<<<<<< ` and `>>>>>>> ` — the `=======` lines in DOCUMENTATION.md are section separators, not conflict markers
- feature/hjt-* branches have unrelated git histories — use --allow-unrelated-histories; they conflict on .gitignore and README.md (keep HHH version, append unique content)
- rapid_dev branch structure: API-service/ (Python FastAPI recommender), habit-recommendation-system-ui/ (Vue UI), mobile/ (Flutter app), app/ (Node.js backend)
